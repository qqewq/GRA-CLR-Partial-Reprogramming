# Assumptions, Limitations, and Non-Claims

## Assumptions
1. Candidate fields are abstract natural-number scores, not biological measurements.
2. Hard constraints are externally supplied and are not biologically calibrated here.
3. No OSK/OSKM expression schedules are modeled.
4. Proofs are conditional on definitions and values supplied to Lean.
5. No experimental dataset is included.

## Limitations
- No cell-state dynamics, pharmacokinetics, expression model, tissue model, or teratoma-risk model.
- No parameter calibration, uncertainty distribution, or biological validation.
- No clinical conclusion can be drawn.

## Non-claims
This repository does not claim universal OSK safety, a universal OSKM safe window, any safe dose or schedule, biological safety certification by Lean, improved reprogramming outcomes, or clinical validation.

Every future biological parameter needs provenance, units, species/tissue context, measurement method, uncertainty, applicability limits, and independent review.
