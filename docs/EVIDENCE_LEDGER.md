# Evidence Ledger

No biological parameter is calibrated in this repository.

| Claim | Status | Evidence required | Permitted wording |
|---|---|---|---|
| GRA generates alternative hypotheses | Proposed architecture | Implementation and benchmark | "The framework proposes..." |
| CLR preserves encoded constraints | Formal contract design | Lean build and proof review | "The abstract contract encodes..." |
| Lean proves biological safety | Unsupported | Cannot follow from formal proof alone | Do not claim |
| OSK is universally safe | Unsupported | Context-specific evidence | Do not claim |
| OSKM has a universal safe window | Unsupported | Context-specific evidence | Do not claim |
| ER-100 proves systemic rejuvenation | Unsupported | Clinical efficacy and long-term evidence | Do not claim |
| Framework improves outcomes | Untested | Comparative benchmark | "To be evaluated" |

For every future biological claim, record full citation, DOI/registry ID, study type, species/population, tissue/cell type, intervention context, endpoint, sample size, limitations, and source status (peer-reviewed, registry, preprint, company announcement).
